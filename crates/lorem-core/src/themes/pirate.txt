The salty crew hoisted the black flag and sailed for the far horizon. A weathered captain studied the treasure map by flickering lantern light. The first mate spotted a merchant galleon riding low with cargo in the morning mist. Cannons rolled to the gunwales as the ship came about with the wind.
Gold doubloons spilled from the chest onto the sandy floor of the hidden cove. The parrot squawked a warning from the rigging high above the deck. Old sea dogs traded tales of the kraken over mugs of grog in the tavern. A storm battered the hull while the helmsman lashed himself to the wheel.
The quartermaster divided the plunder fair and square among the loyal crew. An island rose from the haze with palm trees swaying over white sand. The cabin boy climbed the mast to the crow's nest and scanned the endless blue. Buried treasure waits beneath the old fig tree where the map marks the spot.
The navigator charted a course through the reef by the light of the stars. Barnacles crusted the hull after long months upon the open sea. The crew careened the ship in a sheltered lagoon and patched her timbers. A rival flag appeared on the horizon and the captain called all hands on deck.
The bosun whistled orders as the sails unfurled and caught the trade winds. Smugglers ran the blockade under cover of fog with lanterns dark. The galley cook served hardtack and salted pork to the grumbling watch. Legends speak of a ghost ship that haunts these waters when the moon is full.
The anchor dropped in the harbor as gulls wheeled over the bustling port. Maps and compasses cluttered the captain's table beside a brass spyglass. The crew sang shanties while they hauled the lines through the long afternoon. Freedom calls from beyond the horizon where no flag rules the waves.
